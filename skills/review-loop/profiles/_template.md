# Profile: <name>

A profile supplies the **audience**. The procedure in [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md) supplies the rules for weighing evidence, and those do not change.

**No existing profile will fit a new genre.** That is expected. It was tested twice, and both times the loop structure carried over and the cast did not. Copy the closest profile, delete its cast and house rules, and fill in the sections below. It takes about twenty minutes, and you can reuse the result indefinitely.

---

## Success question

> One sentence. The reader must satisfy it, and you must be able to tell whether
> they did.

Choose the shape by how the audience reads the artifact:

- **Read start to finish:** *"Can they say X in their own words, from memory, after one pass?"*
- **Consulted in fragments:** *"Can they complete <named task> with a verifiable outcome, looking things up as they go?"* Recall is the wrong test here. The task passing is the measurement, and every failed lookup is a finding.

Make it about the reader's **task**, not the document's contents. If you cannot write it, stop. You do not yet know what the work is for.

## House rules the artifact must obey

**Rules with a test attached.** Write "every step has one checkable action", not "steps should be clear". A reader must be able to go and look rather than form an opinion. Three to six rules is usually right.

Examples of the shape, from other genres: *every warning appears before the step it guards*; *every term is glossed at first use*; *every example runs against the live schema*; *a non-detection is never written as an equality*.

## Materials bundle

Say what each kind of reader is handed, by absolute path, and in the form the audience actually meets it. Say which known artifacts of the extraction or rendering are **deliberate**, so that readers do not file them as defects. For a large artifact, say which slice each lens gets and what you left out.

## Cast

**A starting cast, not a fixed one.** Before each round, ask *what did I just change, what am I afraid of, and who would notice?* Then write a reader for each answer. The standing readers are in SKILL.md and apply to every genre, and the disposition auditor runs every round.

## Out of scope

Say what readers should not report, and why. This keeps the tier-3 pile small. It usually includes the quality of the underlying work, genuine open questions that the artifact correctly leaves open, and anything a reader cannot support from the materials.

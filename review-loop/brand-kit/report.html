Review of the brand-kit skill

Two rounds, 2026-10-28. Installed at skills/brand-kit on the branch brand-kit-skill. This review covers coherence (do the files agree with themselves and with the office?) and comprehension (can a fresh agent follow it?). It can't judge merit, meaning whether this is a good way to make a brand. That part is yours.

The stakes. Agents here will follow this skill for a long job with you, over many rounds. Stop when no reader finds a contradiction, or a step an agent couldn't act on. That's where it is now.
